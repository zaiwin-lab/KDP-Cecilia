# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sarawak SME business owners (30s–50s, mixed digital literacy, operating in food/retail/services/crafts across 12+ districts). Primary task: complete a 7-question digital audit in under 3 minutes and receive their RM1,000 coupon eligibility.

Secondary audience: government officials and programme directors previewing the portal to approve its rollout — they need to see credibility, polish, and programme legitimacy at first glance.

## Product Purpose

A lead-capture audit portal for the Sarawak Digital Champion (KSDC/KDP) programme. It qualifies SME owners for a government-backed RM1,000 digital coupon redeemable against a professional website build. Cecilia (KSDC programme officer) uses the admin dashboard to manage leads and send payment links.

## Brand Personality

Official, approachable, action-oriented.

The portal must feel like a real government digital initiative — not a freelancer landing page or a SaaS sign-up. It earns trust from the government officials who will endorse it, and it does not intimidate the SME owners who will use it.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (Notion-adjacent, editorial serif + italic, Klim-inspired)
- Cream-and-muted-slate AI default
- Bureaucratic government PDF dumps
- Marketplace-style forms (Shopee/Lazada checkout energy)
- Startup pitch decks

## Design Principles

1. **Programme legitimacy first.** Every design choice should make a government official nod, not cringe. Restraint where restraint signals authority; colour where colour signals energy.
2. **The coupon is the promise.** RM1,000 is the whole value proposition. It should feel real, tangible, and worth something — not a UI decoration.
3. **One screen, one job.** Each of the 7 questions gets its own moment. No clutter, no sidebar, no distraction.
4. **Demo mode is a first-class feature.** Government bosses test things. The demo path must be frictionless and complete.
5. **Credibility through completeness.** Empty states, loading states, and the result screen must all feel finished, not scaffolded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on all surfaces. Reduced-motion alternative for all animations. Bahasa Malaysia primary language (forms + UI). Works on mid-range Android phones with 4G.
